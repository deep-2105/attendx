# AttendX Analytics Notes — September 27, 2026

AttendX analytics are intended to turn attendance records into understandable summaries.

## Professor Analytics

Professor-facing analytics can summarize attendance across students and provide trends or present/absent distributions for administrative review.

## Student Analytics

Student-facing analytics should focus on the authenticated student's own attendance information and progress.

## Consistency

Analytics should derive from the same attendance records used by attendance and reporting screens so that changes remain consistent across the application.

## Privacy

Analytics must respect the same role and database access boundaries as the underlying attendance records.
