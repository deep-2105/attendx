# AttendX Reporting Notes — September 27, 2026

## Purpose

Reports provide a structured view of attendance information for academic review.

## Consistency

Report totals and percentages should use the same underlying attendance records as the attendance workflow.

## Professor Access

Professor reports may summarize permitted student attendance information for administrative purposes.

## Student Access

Student reporting views should remain limited to the authenticated student's permitted information.

## Export

Any export functionality should be reviewed for accidental inclusion of information outside the current user's authorization boundary.
