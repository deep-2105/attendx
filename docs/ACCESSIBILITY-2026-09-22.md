# AttendX Accessibility Notes

AttendX interfaces should remain readable and usable across common screen sizes.

- Keep navigation labels descriptive.
- Maintain visible active, hover and keyboard-focus states.
- Keep text contrast readable against cards and navigation backgrounds.
- Give form controls clear labels and useful validation feedback.
- Avoid making important information depend only on color.
- Keep tables, charts and dashboards usable on narrow screens.
- Distinguish loading, empty, error and authorization states clearly.
